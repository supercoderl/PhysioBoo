WHO WE ARE

Healthcare information, made easier to navigate

PhysioBoo is an online platform for exploring healthcare options. It brings information about doctors, healthcare services and clinics into one place, so you can compare what is available before deciding where to go.

PhysioBoo does not provide medical treatment itself. Care is given by the doctors and clinics listed on the platform, and they confirm any appointment you request.
